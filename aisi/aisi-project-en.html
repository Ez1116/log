AISI Implementation Cases

Demonstrating AISI platform's educational innovation achievements through real teaching field applications

Implementation Case (1)

Fuhe Junior High x Media Play Media Literacy

Innovative teaching centered on media literacy

Fuhe Junior High's school-based curriculum "Media Play Media Literacy" is centered on media literacy, combining reading literacy and information ethics to cultivate students' critical thinking and digital citizenship. It focuses on mass media and social media literacy, covering issues such as news reading, fake news identification, influencer phenomena, and headline manipulation.

🎯 Innovation Highlights

Teacher Meifeng introduced the AISI system paired with the "Think Forge" platform, providing immediate prompts and learning suggestions through AI dialogue to help students clarify their thinking and reconstruct logic.

This digital-assisted design realizes the integration of "teaching, learning, and assessment," making AI a learning partner that guides students from passive reception to active participation, building thoughtful modern media consumers.

Implementation Case (2)

Fuhe Junior High x SDGs Camp

Cross-age differentiated learning support

AISI platform's cross-age support capability is an important feature, providing differentiated support for different learning stages. While junior high students have learning motivation and curiosity, they lack immediate appropriate feedback due to insufficient experience and often don't understand true learning during the early stages of autonomous learning ability cultivation.

🌱 Learning Support

AISI serves as a diverse learning consultant, providing alternative experiences and knowledge to guide students in becoming responsible social citizens.

The AISI team designed autonomous learning courses integrating science, technology, and civil society using "Net Zero Carbon Emissions" as SDGs learning materials.

Implementation Case (3)

Tucheng Junior High x Science in Titanic

Contextualized learning of abstract concepts

Junior high students entering the formal operational stage begin using abstract concepts to explain scientific phenomena, but often become observers in class due to complex concepts, losing direction during autonomous learning. AISI has highly adaptive and real-time response characteristics, allowing teachers to adjust response modes according to curriculum needs.

🔬 Scientific Reasoning

Covering aspects such as reasoning argumentation and critical thinking, using AISI to gradually guide students in internalizing abstract concepts.

Teacher Shengyi used the Titanic iceberg collision incident as the theme, deeply integrating mechanics concepts that junior high students often find difficult into contextual scenarios, allowing students to explore abstract scientific concepts during the Titanic's voyage, sinking process, and post-sinking through reasoning argumentation, critical thinking, model building, and other cognitive skills.

Implementation Case (4)

Neihu Senior High x Enhancing Problem-Solving and Inquiry Abilities

AI-assisted learning for scientific inquiry

While high school students have a foundation in scientific knowledge, they lack hands-on operational opportunities and insufficient practice in problem-solving skills. Even when schools provide inquiry and practice opportunities, students still face difficulties such as not knowing how to start, adjust, or evaluate problem-solving effectiveness.

🤖 AI Scientific Role Models

AISI features built-in scientific inquiry chatbots that play expert role models from different fields, helping students analyze problem structures and practice the application of scientific methods.

High-productivity communicative interaction relies heavily on problem awareness and critical thinking. Unlike general generative AI that simply obtains information, Teacher Yichen's inquiry and practice courses encourage students to actively try and personally experience the differences between AI suggestions and their own ideas, practicing interaction with technology while evaluating new and old knowledge, simultaneously developing both soft and hard skills.
